# 🛒 E-Commerce Customer & Sales Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end E-Commerce Sales Analytics solution developed using **SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI**. It analyzes customer purchasing behavior, product performance, revenue trends, and market insights to help businesses make data-driven decisions.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation using RFM analysis, SQL-based business analysis, interactive Power BI dashboards, and executive-level business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across products and countries.
- Identify top-performing products and customers.
- Perform customer segmentation using RFM Analysis.
- Track revenue trends and seasonal sales.
- Build executive dashboards for business decision-making.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- United Kingdom generated the highest revenue.
- Revenue peaked during November and December.
- A small percentage of customers generated most of the revenue.
- VIP customers contributed significantly to overall sales.
- A few products accounted for a large share of total revenue.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Retain VIP customers using loyalty programs.
- Increase marketing campaigns during Q4.
- Focus inventory planning on top-selling products.
- Expand operations in high-performing countries.
- Improve customer retention through personalized offers.
